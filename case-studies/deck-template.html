Rebuilding
Afterpay Design

Rebuilding a design discipline as a strategic function during a regulatory pivot and a rebrand.

Block (Afterpay)  //  2024–2025  //  Design Director

Context

Situation

A team of two navigating a regulatory pivot.

The Afterpay design team had shrunk to 2 people. The business was navigating a regulatory pivot across AU, UK, and US markets while simultaneously rebranding under Block.

Goal

Rebuild trust, team, and throughput.

Rebuild the team, establish design as a trusted strategic partner, and ship regulated product changes without slowing delivery.

The Goal

Design as a trusted strategic partner — not just a delivery resource.

Approach

01

Hiring & team structure

Grew from 2 to 7 designers across 3 markets, embedded directly in product squads to build influence through proximity and output.

"Hire for craft first — trust follows from doing the work well."

02

Rituals & craft standards

Weekly critique, shared design principles, and a Figma component library rebuilt from scratch for the Block rebrand.

"Consistency compounds — every shared component is a decision you don't have to make twice."

03

Strategic positioning

Design reviews with the CPO, a quarterly design strategy narrative, and a clear RACI that elevated design from executor to author.

"Speak in outcomes. Earn the room. Then hold the line."

Output

Figma component library — 2025 rebrand-ready

Impact

2→7 Designers Hired 5 designers across AU, UK & US
+36% YoY GMV Gross merchandise value growth
1.5–6× Referral lift On Shop across markets
3 Markets shipped AU, UK & US regulatory releases

Outcome categories

Product

  • Compliance-ready component library

Business

Scale

Reflection

We did it by showing up consistently.
